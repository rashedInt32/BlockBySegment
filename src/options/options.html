Good to know

  • A browser extension can't stop a determined child — they could remove the extension, use another browser, or a guest profile. Pair this with a supervised Chrome profile / Family Link, and consider enabling this extension in Incognito too (chrome://extensions → Details).
  • Time only counts while the site is the active tab, the browser is focused, and the computer isn't idle.
  • Segments are equal slices of the day from midnight: e.g. 4 segments = 6-hour blocks at 12am, 6am, 12pm, 6pm.
